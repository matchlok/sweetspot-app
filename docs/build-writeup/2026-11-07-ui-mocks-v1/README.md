# UI mocks v1 (2026-11-07)

First mocks from the UX/PM brainstorm (BMAD party mode: Sally and John). Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/FFY3jjpET1Nbd96jPdjXF9 (private). Numbers are sample data.

- `canvas-v1.png`: screenshot of the canvas before the first round of feedback. Rendered locally from `source/` with `snapshot.html`.
- `source/`: the artboard files as they were at that point.

## What led here

- The 6 a.m. message is the front door, not a 9:15 p.m. review. Evening check-ins felt like a report card and got ignored. Narjit's phone window is 6–8 a.m.; then focus until 4 p.m.
- Replan proposes, never moves. Inputs: actual ride-time history, family calendar (read-only), temperature (50°F confidence drops, below 40°F trainer or skip), daylight, trainer-OK tag per workout.
- Slack ruled out: work Slack lives on the same phone.
- AI-accuracy analytics go in a separate "Sweetspot Ops" view, not the athlete app. The public demo comes from Ops later.

## Feedback on v1

- Keep: the push notification (1).
- 2a Today: dark direction is right, but flat (no contrast between sections). The "Needs attention" panel reads as AI boilerplate. Copy should sound like a coach, not an assistant. The week bar didn't show zones.
- 2b Today (light): dropped. Boring, no insight.
- 3 Replan: keep the options, add "something else" free text/voice to Claude.
- 4 Week: "On track, one gap" reads as AI copy. Zone colors: Z2 blue, Z3 green, Z4 yellow, Z5 red.
- IA: Today → Home icon; Week → Calendar icon that zooms out to month; Plan → an actionable button, not a tab.
